YEAR: 2025

- Oct 23 — Grades due for 1st 7-Week
- Oct 28 — Last day to register for 2nd 7-Week/Disney
- Oct 29 — 2nd 7-Week begins (Drop Day)

Late Term
- Nov 5 — W begins (2nd 7-Week)
- Nov 12 — Last day to withdraw (Regular & Later Start)
- Nov 26 — No credit classes; offices open

- Dec 2 — Last day to withdraw (2nd 7-Week)
- Dec 19 — Last day of semester
- Dec 21 — Grades due (Fall + 2nd 7-Week)
