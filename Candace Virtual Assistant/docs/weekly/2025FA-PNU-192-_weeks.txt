Additional Information
Written Exams:
This course will utilize four exams, final exam, and ATI Fundamentals proctored exams
as forms of assessment.
Discussions:
Students will be expected to take part in two online discussion about various topics
related to the course, which will be uploaded in canvas. Directions on how to participate
in the discussion will be provided in canvas. No late submissions will be accepted for
discussion assignment after the due date for the response post for each discussion
assignment. Please use the information present to craft an original response to the
question posed in the discussion.
Failure Policy:
No retakes on written quizzes or exams. Student requires a course average of 75 or
better to move forward in the program. Students are expected to take exams on the
scheduled dates for the course. In the event of an emergency necessitating an absence,
such illness or death in the family, students must notify the course instructor as soon as
possible. In the event of an absence from an exam, the student is required to present
documentation that verifies the emergency circumstances. Missed exams will be
rescheduled at the convenience of the course instructor. The makeup exam may vary
from the original test. The missed exam will be scheduled within seven (7) days of the
original exam date, or the student will receive an exam grade of "0".
Professionalism:
Attendance, participation, and behavior are expected to meet the standards of
professional performance which will be evaluated on the following criteria: Time
management, Respect, Participation, and Quality of work. A rubric will be used to grade
professionalism and it will be provided to all students during the first lecture. Students
must complete all assignments and will be asked to bring certificates as entrance to lab.
Academic Assistance:
Any student who is having trouble with this course or requires special consideration or
assistance is encouraged to seek assistance within the first few weeks of class or as
soon as the need arises. Problems and exceptions should be dealt with in advance and
in private. Individualized attention and assistance is available, but you must ask for them.
