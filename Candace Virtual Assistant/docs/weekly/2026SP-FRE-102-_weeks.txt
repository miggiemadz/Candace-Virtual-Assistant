Suggested Teaching Methodologies
1. Through oral and written activities, the student develops communications skills and
mastery of grammatical concepts.
2. Since language and culture are inextricably bound together, cultural understanding should
be developed along with the four language skills.
3. The instructor will mostly use French as the language of instruction. He/she will model and
reinforce the proper use of the language through classwork and through use of relevant
online materials such as YouTube videos, online oral/aural activities or French websites.
4. A final oral presentation by each student and informal forms of assessment (such as
asking/answering questions in class) will enable students to build on their French
knowledge step by step.
5. Techniques of instruction will include short lecture or presentation of material (10-15
minutes), classroom discussions, group work, role-playing guided dictations and student
oral presentations.
