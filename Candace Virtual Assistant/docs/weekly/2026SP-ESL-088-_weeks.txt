Experiential Learning
Students must complete an experiential learning activity that connects course content to
career applications. This activity may be a content specific assignment or practical skill
that is applied within a course assignment. This assignment supports the general
education learning outcomes of scientific/critical thinking and quantitative reasoning; oral
and written communication; and information literacy/technological competency.

Suggested Teaching Methodologies
1. ESL 088 Reading/Listening/Speaking will coordinate academic themes with ESL 087
Grammar/Writing grammatical structures and communication skills to provide students
opportunities to develop clarity and fluency in their writing and speaking. ESL 088 will allow
students to expand their language use through activities that incorporate experiential
learning into the curriculum.
2. Students will be introduced to the themes of family life, substance abuse, homelessness,
and child abuse which correspond to themes present in both the novel and the reading
text. To achieve clarity and fluency students will respond in writing through journals and
free writings. Students will also listen to academic texts and lectures, utilize note taking
strategies, and participate in classroom discussions.
3. ESL 087 and ESL 088 Reading/Listening/Speaking will also coordinate with ESL 089 CALL
to provide students with individualized practice in communication and written skills.
