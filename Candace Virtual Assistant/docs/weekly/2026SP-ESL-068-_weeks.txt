Suggested Teaching Methodologies
Coordinating ESL 068 Reading/Academic Listening and Speaking with ESL 067
Writing/Grammar provides students with opportunities to develop clarity and fluency in writing and
speaking and to expand their language skills and content knowledge that are taught in each
course. Whenever possible, ESL 068 Reading/Academic Listening and Speaking should aim to
incorporate skills and content from writing/grammar class.
1. Journals/Response Writings (uploaded into Canvas or on paper) will be kept for both reading
and listening. In the responses, students will explore and analyze themes and concepts from
readings and lectures and relate them to their own experiences and points of view.
2. Students will spend time on theme-based assignments, which integrate all language skills,
such as research and an oral presentation on a theme related to the reading and/or listening. An
alternative oral presentation topic could be career information.
3. Wherever possible, concepts and themes from the reading will be enhanced through listening
segments of like content. Notetaking skills will be drawn from listening segments from textbooks,
internet, and films.
4. Students will have opportunities for structured class discussions and debates based on themes
from the reading and listening.
5. Students will work collaboratively in small groups, which can include out-of-class Canvas
discussion groups.
6. Students will incorporate sources such as databases and web sites into at least one research
project related to course content.
7. Students will spend one class and some out-of-class time researching information about
majors and careers. They will develop an academic schedule and/or an overview plan for
subsequent semesters.
