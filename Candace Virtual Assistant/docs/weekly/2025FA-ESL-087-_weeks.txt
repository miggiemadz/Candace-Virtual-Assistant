Additional Information
The Academic Learning Center in the Kellogg Building offers free tutoring both in-person
and virtually. It is also a great place to do homework, with tutors available to help. Since
this is an accelerated course, it is recommended that all students visit the Academic
Learning Center to get extra practice and gain a deeper understanding of course
material.
Experiential Learning
Students must complete an experiential learning activity that connects course content to
career applications. This activity may be a content specific assignment or practical skill
that is applied within a course assignment. This assignment supports the general
education learning outcomes of scientific/critical thinking and quantitative reasoning; oral
and written communication; and information literacy/technological competency.

Suggested Teaching Methodologies
1. ESL 087 Grammar/Writing and ESL 088 Reading/Listening/Speaking will coordinate
grammatical structures and communication skills with academic themes to provide
students opportunities to develop clarity and fluency in their writing and speaking. ESL 087
will allow students to expand their language use through activities that incorporate
experiential learning into the curriculum.
2. In ESL 087 students will be introduced to the organization of an academic essay and the
writing process through revising and editing of compositions. Students will also do in-class
writings, journals, responses, or free writings and maintain a portfolio of these writing
activities.
3. Grammar is taught in context with the emphasis on students’ incorporating the structures
into written and oral communicative activities. To enhance clarity and fluency students will
respond in writing and in discussions to academic texts and lectures. By the end of the
semester, students will speak and write about their experiences and textbook themes with
high intermediate fluency and grammatical accuracy.
4. ESL 087 Grammar/Writing will also coordinate with ESL 089 CALL to provide students with
individualized practice in communication and written skills.
