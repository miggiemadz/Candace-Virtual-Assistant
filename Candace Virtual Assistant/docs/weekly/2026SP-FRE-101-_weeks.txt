Suggested Teaching Methodologies
1. Through oral and written activities, the student develops communication skills and masters
grammatical concepts.
2. Since language and culture are inextricably bound together, cultural understanding should
be developed along with the four language skills.
3. The instructor will mostly use French as the language of instruction and will model and
reinforce the proper use of the language through classwork and also through use of
relevant online materials such as YouTube videos, online aural activities or French
websites.
4. A final oral presentation by each student and informal forms of assessments (such as
asking/answering questions in class) will enable students to build on their French
knowledge step by step.
5. Techniques of instruction will include short lecture or presentation of material (10-15
minutes), classroom discussions, group work, role-playing, guided dictations, and student
oral presentations.
