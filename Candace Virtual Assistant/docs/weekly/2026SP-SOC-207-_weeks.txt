Suggested Teaching Methodologies
The primary goal of this course is to enable the student to become familiar with an
overview of the study of social inequalities that exist in our society, including racism,
sexism, ageism and a number of other types of inequalities based on religion, ethnicity,
physical or mental disabilities etc. In addition, the goal is to establish an exchange of
information obtained from the required text materials and the students’ direct
observations of attempts to resolve or solve these inequities.
