Class Policies
1. Participation: The faculty view attending course lectures either face-to-face or online
as an integral part of the program and a critical step towards successful completion of
each course. It is expected that each student will attend all lectures, laboratory and
clinical sessions, completing all assignments/activities. This includes reporting for class
on-time and not be in the college laboratory or testing center during class time. All
students must attend applicable classroom, online, laboratory and clinical orientation
sessions as assigned. Virtual attendance is defined as video ON during class virtual
lectures.
2. Laboratory sessions are a mandatory (6) hours per week according to the student’s
scheduled laboratory section.
3. All students will attend the assigned clinical rotations as discussed with the Director of
Clinical Education and Program Director each week during the semester as specified on
Monday / Tuesday (7a-3p) only unless additional required sessions are required and
offered.
4. Attendance at weekly laboratory and clinical sessions are required.
5 . Students are not to attend alternate laboratory sessions or an alternative clinical
affiliate site without permission from the DCE or PD.
5. Students are also expected to go to open lab skill sessions on their own to practice the
skills demonstrated in laboratory sessions.
7. Students are required to attend at least 1 hour of open lab weekly in addition to their
regularly scheduled laboratory section attendance.
8. Laboratory assignments and skill demonstration due dates will be provided as
applicable by laboratory instructors.
9. Students are expected to be on time for all laboratory and clinical sessions . At the
discretion of laboratory instructors, late arrivals 10 minutes past the start of the lab will be
sent home and the lab will need to be made up. Mandatory documentation is required if a
student misses a lab.
10. Dress code: For all laboratory, clinical laboratory or clinical rotation sessions,
Respiratory Care Program specified Short Lab Coat and Student College ID must be
worn at all times. No flip-flops, open toe shoes, or shorts allowed in the lab. Also, please
refer to RCP Student Handbook Dress Code policy.
11. Cellphone use: cellphones are permitted in the classroom, however they must not be
a distraction so they must be silenced, placed on “do not disturb” mode as well as away
in a bag. They should not be on the student desk. If they are found out, or in use, the
faculty reserves the right to put the cellphone at the instructor’s lectern till the end of
lecture sessions when they will be returned. If the device is considered disruptive or if the
student refuses to comply with faculty request(s), the student will be asked to remove
themselves from the lecture
Experiential Learning
Students must complete an experiential learning activity that connects course content to
career applications. This activity may be a content specific assignment or practical skill
that is applied within a course assignment. This assignment supports the general
education learning outcomes of scientific/critical thinking and quantitative reasoning; oral
and written communication; and information literacy/technological competency.

Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
UCNJ Union College of Union County, NJ offers reasonable accommodations and/or
services to persons with disabilities. Any student who has a documented disability and
wishes to self-identify should contact the Director of Universal Accessibility Services and
Veterans Affairs at (908) 709-7164, or email accessibility@ucc.edu. Accommodations
are individualized and in accordance with Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973
and the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1992. In order to receive accommodations,
students must be registered with Disability Support Services. Students should register
with the office as soon as possible. Accommodations are not official until the Faculty
Accommodations Alert Form(s) are issued from the student to his/her instructor(s).
